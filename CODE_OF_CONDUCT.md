# Code of conduct

TurnOver is a small project run in spare time. To keep it a place people want to come back to:

- Be kind, patient and specific. Assume good intent.
- Criticise the work, never the person.
- No harassment, insults, or personal attacks, and nothing that demeans anyone for disability, race, gender,
  sexuality, religion, age or anything else about who they are.
- Nobody is owed a reply, a fix or a feature. Asking is welcome; demanding isn't.

The maintainer may edit, hide or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break these rules, and may block
people who keep breaking them. To report a problem privately, use GitHub's "Report content" on the comment or issue.
